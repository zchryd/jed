Planning the day
  • Fog runs the schedule: summer mornings are often gray on the coast and sunny in Hiouchi — do forest and river mornings, coast afternoons; sunset from Pebble Beach or Radio Road.
  • Tides run the coast: tidepooling and the Battery Point crossing both need a low tide — check a Crescent City tide table the night before.
  • No cell service in most of the park and along Howland Hill Road — download or print before leaving Hiouchi; fuel up in Crescent City or Gasquet.
  • Sneaker waves are the real hazard on every beach here: never turn your back on the ocean, keep children well up the beach.
