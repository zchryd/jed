JEDEDIAH SMITH REDWOODS — MAPS & HISTORY
=========================================

WHAT THIS IS
A researched, fact-checked guide to Jedediah Smith Redwoods State Park,
the Smith River, the campground, and the surrounding Del Norte County
area — as three web pages you open in your browser. No internet needed
for the basics; aerial photos appear when you're online.

HOW TO OPEN IT
Double-click "Start Here.html" — it links to everything.
(Or use the "Jed Smith Park Maps" shortcut in your open-shortcuts folder.)

THE FIVE PAGES
1. Historical Map — 26 historical events pinned on a real map:
   Tolowa Dee-ni' villages and history, Jedediah Smith's five days here
   in June 1828, the 1955 and 1964 floods, the Brother Jonathan wreck,
   how the park was made, and more. Click pins or the timeline list.
   Buttons at the bottom-left switch the background (aerial photo /
   USGS topo map / plain offline view).

2. Campground Map & Notebook — every campsite (1–106 and A–E), cabins,
   restrooms, showers, the summer footbridge to Stout Grove.
   • Type a site number in the search box to jump to it.
   • Click any site and "Save note" to attach your own note to it
     (e.g. "shady, close to the river — book this one").
   • "Drop a note pin" lets you mark any spot with a note.
   • Notes save themselves on this computer. "Save notes to file"
     backs them up; "Load notes" brings them back.

3. The History, Verified — the full story as one readable report,
   with every date checked and sources listed at the end.

4. Field Guide — what lives where and how to identify it: the big
   trees told apart in seconds, the understory, salmon and lamprey,
   newts (don't lick), tidepool creatures, mushrooms (look, don't
   eat), organized by the nine habitats you'll actually walk through.
   Includes the safety short-list and the collecting rules.

5. Hikes & Day Trips — every park trail with its official mileage
   (Stout Grove to Little Bald Hills), the Grove of Titans boardwalk,
   plus the Crescent City ring, the coast south on 101, and the
   swimming holes up 199 — thirty destinations on one clickable map,
   each with a practical tip (tides, permits, parking).

GOOD TO KNOW
• Campsite positions are approximate — placed along the real campground
  roads to match the official State Parks map. The official printed maps
  (PDF) are in the "official-maps" folder.
• Where history is contested (for example the death tolls of the 1853
  Yontocket and 1854 Achulet massacres), the pages show the range of
  figures and who reports them, rather than one smoothed-over number.
• Campground reservations: parks.ca.gov or (800) 444-7275.

HOW THE FACTS WERE CHECKED
130 claims were gathered from 26 sources (mostly primary documents:
1828 expedition journals, USGS flood reports, the 1990 federal statute,
National Park Service records, Tolowa Dee-ni' Nation publications).
Each fact was then independently re-checked by reviewers instructed to
try to disprove it, and four disputed dates were resolved against
primary records. Where sources still disagree, the pages say so.
